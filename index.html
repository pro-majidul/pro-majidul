Inspiration

This song was inspired by Pablo Neruda's poem "Ode to the Butterfly," written in the 1950s. In the poem, a man gallops with a friend across Argentina's wide plains—with mountains in the distance—when a cloud of butterflies darkens the sky, nearly blotting out the sun.

They swarm through the fields, leaving crops in ruins, and the man on the horse, alarmed by the devastation, raises his voice against them: "You shall not attack man and his legacy, the peasant and his animals."

Reading this, this songwriter was struck by his defense of man's legacy and asked herself: Is humanity's legacy something I am proud of today? The answer was no—and this song was born, casting a spell of renewal and reimagining our shared legacy.
"A transformation born from poetic reflection"

Invitation

"My Spell" is a poetic spell of self-recognition and empowerment. It's an audacious look at our society and its legacy, during this season when spells are cast and energy shifts—not a dark spell, but one of transformation and renewal.

Opportunities await for stepping into a vital and visible new way of living.
